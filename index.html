Method First

The repo separates methodology from conclusions. That prevents a public artifact from overstating results before source links, retrieval dates, and permission notes are complete.

  1. Collect public filings and sustainability reports.
  2. Classify adaptation, governance, financing, and operational action.
  3. Run transparent scoring before deeper qualitative review.
